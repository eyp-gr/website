Who We Are

Get to Know the European Youth Parliament Greece

The European Youth Parliament Greece (EYP Greece) is a non-profit organization and independent of political and party interests. It was established in 1994 as one of the 40 National Committees of the European Parliament, with its permanent seat in Thessaloniki.

Our Goals

The main goals of the organization are the promotion of a European dimension in the education of young people, taking on initiatives, as well as the development of social and professional skills.
