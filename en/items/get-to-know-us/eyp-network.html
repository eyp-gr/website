The European Youth Parliament

The EYP Network

The European Youth Parliament (EYP) is a network of organisations active across Europe, headquartered in Berlin, and comprising 41 national committees. Since its foundation in 1987, it has aimed to promote the European dimension in education. To achieve this goal, it organises more than 100 activities, involving over 25,000 young people every year. It is a non-profit organisation that operates independently of political and party interests.
