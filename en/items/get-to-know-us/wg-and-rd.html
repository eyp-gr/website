Working Groups and Regional Groups

Working Groups

Working Groups are composed of members of the EYP Greece with an interest in the theme of the Group and are usually selected after their applications, by the Board Member who is responsible for the Group.

Regional Groups

Regional Groups are a new structure within the EYP Greece, which aims to strengthen the presence of the organization throughout Greece. Regional Groups serve as the link between the EYP Greece and the local communities of the country, so that we can enhance participation and accessibility of the organization.
