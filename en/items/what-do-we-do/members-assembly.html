Members' Assemblies

Members' Assemblies of the organisation are held according to the EYP Greece charter once a month. Members' Assemblies, as stated in Article 13 of the Charter, aim at the analysis of issues that have arisen within the National Committee.

In these assemblies, all individuals who are interested in getting to know the EYP Greece have the right to participate, regardless of whether they are financially up-to-date members for the current year. Nevertheless, the total number of financially up-to-date members also holds the right to vote on the topics to be discussed.
