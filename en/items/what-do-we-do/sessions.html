Conferences and Forums

Regional Conferences

EYP Greece organizes Regional Conferences at regular intervals in various cities of Greece, with the goal of promoting the organization in the regions and strengthening the participation of young people in European affairs.

National Conferences

EYP Greece organizes two National Selection Conferences each year, one in Athens and one in Thessaloniki. In these conferences, students participate in a full four-day program simulating the work of the European Parliament, and those who excel, participate in international conferences and forums, representing Greece.

International Sessions & Forums

Apart from the National Conferences, EYP Greece has successfully organized international events, such as the 100th International Session held in Thessaloniki in 2024 and numerous international forums.

International Trainings

EYP Greece organizes training programs (International Training) that aim to train the entire network, so that they can take on official roles in Conferences and Forums. The 3rd International Training, held in March 2026 in New Kallikrateia, Chalkidiki, is the most recent International Training organized by EYP Greece.
