Mentorship Program

Every new or existing member of the European Youth Parliament Greece (EYP Greece) may face certain challenges in understanding how the organisation operates, integrating smoothly into it, and finding the right “steps” to follow on their journey.

The EYP Greece “Touched by an Angel” Mentorship Programme offers a solution to these challenges. It is coordinated by the Human Resources Working Group (Coordinator: Maria Erotokritou; Members: Melina Machimari, Lena Spanou, Thanasis Karageorgos, Dimitra Gardiki, Anastasia Tzika, and Alexandra Tampakou).

Who is it for?

In practice, the programme is open to all members and friends of EYP Greece who have started or would like to start their journey in the organisation, as well as to members who want to continue that journey creatively and productively.



More specifically, the Mentorship Programme aims to support the smooth integration of:

  • Students who have been involved with EYP Greece in the past but did not continue their journey actively,
  • Members and friends who want guidance on the next steps they should take and wish to deepen their knowledge of the organisation’s administrative matters,
  • Students who have recently learned about EYP Greece and have not participated in any of its sessions.
