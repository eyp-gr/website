Become a Partner

Why should you partner with EYP Greece

  • Impact:
    You will contribute to the organization of actions that will provide a unique experience for hundreds of young people and high school students who participate as volunteers or members.
  • Promotion:
    You will promote your product and/or company to an international audience composed of young people aged 16-25 from all over Europe.
  • Professional Development:
    You will play a significant role in shaping a new generation of citizens, who will be called upon to use the tools they will acquire from our actions in their future professional careers and in the job market.

Ways to Support us

  • Financial Sponsorship:
    Most EYP Greece partners support the sustainability of our activities through financial sponsorship, which covers accommodation, venue rental, as well as the purchase of materials and food for delegates.
  • Material Donations:
    Another way you can support EYP Greece's activities is by donating materials. Material sponsorships usually cover a conference's needs for stationery or computers and audiovisual equipment (microphones, speakers, projectors).
  • Gift Vouchers and Products:
    You can support EYP Greece by donating gift vouchers or products from your company, which will be used to raise funds for the organization and during conferences.
  • Promotion and Collaboration through Social and Mass Media:
    In addition to the above, you can support the organization by promoting it through coordinated outreach on news websites, radio and television stations via press releases and interviews, posts and stories, and cross-promotion of activities on social media.
