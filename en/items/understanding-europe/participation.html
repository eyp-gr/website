The workshops of our program

The educational workshops of our program are specifically designed to promote participatory dialogue and are offered to:

  • Primary Schools (5th & 6th Grade)
  • Secondary Schools
  • Organisations and Institutions with a similar interest in European issues

Participate

There are two ways to get involved and experience the Understanding Europe program:

  1. As a Participant: Start by experiencing the program up close. At this level, you will attend the workshops (crash courses), get familiar with our methodology, and see how participatory teaching works.
  2. As a Trainer: Are you ready to step into the classroom and empower other young people? To become a formal trainer and join our team, the process is simple:
    • Application: Declare your interest in one of our open calls for new trainers.
    • Training for Trainers (T4T): Once your application is accepted, you will attend the specialized training program T4T.
    • Certification: Upon completion, you will be officially certified, join the team, and take on the coordination of your own workshops!
