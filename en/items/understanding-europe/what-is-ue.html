What is the program?

Understanding Europe

Understanding Europe is a pan-European Educational Network that empowers young people in Europe through education for the democratic identity of the citizen and aims to give them a voice. The educational forms and methods of Understanding Europe are based on the approach of peer-to-peer/young people education. Through this structure, Understanding Europe approaches students, mainly from high schools, in many European countries, and adapts the form of the program according to each country.

Understanding Europe Greece is a program of the European Youth Parliament of Greece (EYP Greece). Through the Understanding Europe Network, we wish to strengthen our contribution to the participation of young people, using our experience in projects for youth and European issues, so that we can combat Euroscepticism and cultivate the sense of European identity and "belonging" among students.
