The Structure of Understanding Europe

Educational Approach

The educational approach of the training workshops (Crash Courses) of Understanding Europe focuses on cultivating basic skills that promote a culture of participatory teaching and learning, giving emphasis to inclusion as well as diversity. Through educational workshops provided by young people for young people, the program gives students the opportunity to become active European citizens, participating in an open and pluralistic Europe. This approach encourages students to engage substantively with European issues, providing them with the knowledge and tools they need to contribute positively to their communities and beyond.

The Team

The structure of our team is simple and consists of the following roles:

Coordinators

They are responsible for coordinating the programme at the national level, organising activities, and ensuring the team's smooth cooperation and operation.

Peer Educators

They first receive specialised training themselves, so that they can then take on the essential role of training, guiding, and preparing the next generation of educators.

Trainers

They are the programme's representatives, responsible for preparing and directly delivering the training seminars (crash courses) to students and organisations.
