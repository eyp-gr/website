Alumni Network

Alumni of EYP Greece are considered to be all individuals who have participated in the Organisation's activities and programmes, in any role, but have completed their active involvement in the Network.

The mission of the EYP Greece Alumni Network is to maintain the connection of Alumni both with one another and with the Organisation and its values.

Through the experience and knowledge they have gained from their participation and the roles they have undertaken, Alumni can continue to support our mission and contribute to the work of EYP Greece.

Their lasting connection with the Organisation contributes to the stability and continuity of a network that evolves, inspires and supports the next generations of EYPiers active in our country.
