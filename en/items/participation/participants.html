Participants

High School Students

High school students who are interested in participating in the activities of EYP Greece can participate as delegates in the National Selection Conferences in Athens and Thessaloniki through their school and the responsible teacher, as well as participate in other activities (Regional Conferences, Trainings, and Mentorship).

Information for Schools

Young Adults

Individuals over the age of 18 can participate as officials or mentees in the Mentorship Program by creating an account on the Members Platform and submitting an application for participation in activities throughout Greece and Europe.
