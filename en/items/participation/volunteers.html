Officials Teams

Discover the teams you can join as an official in EYP events across Europe!

Members Platform

Academic Team

The Academic Team is comprised of the President, Vice-Presidents, and Chairpersons. The entire Academic Team is responsible for guiding the delegates in drafting the resolutions and ensuring the smooth conduct of the General Assembly of the action.

Media Team

The Media Team is comprised of the Editor, the Editorial Assistant, and the Media Team Members. The Media team is responsible for covering and promoting the action on Social Media.

Organizing Team

The Organizing Team is comprised of the Head Organizers, the Core Organizers, and the Organizers. The Organizing Team is responsible for the preparation and organization of the action.

Jury

The Jury is responsible for evaluating the participants and compiling the list of delegates who will represent the country in international sessions and other events.

Safety Team

The Safety Team is comprised of the Event Safe Person and the National Safety Person and has the purpose of ensuring the safety of the action and the well-being of its participants.
