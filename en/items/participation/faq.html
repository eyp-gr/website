FAQ

When are the annual conferences held?

April (Athens) and December (Thessaloniki)

How can I stay updated?

You can stay updated about upcoming events and available applications through our website and social media platforms.

How can I participate?

You can participate in several ways, depending on your age and experience:

  • If you are a high school student: through your school and your responsible teacher, at the National or Regional Selection Conferences.
  • If you are over 18: through open applications for officials, participation in HR events or attendance at the Member's Assemblies.
  • If you want to participate in other events: you can apply for participation in Regional Conferences, Trainings or the Mentorship Programme.

How can I stay updated about new events and open applications?

You can stay updated about new events and open applications through our website and social media platforms.

How can I get additional guidance within the organization?

Guidance can be provided through:

  • the Mentorship Programme
  • the Trainings of EYP Greece
  • participation in the Member's Assemblies or
  • communication with members of the Executive Board
